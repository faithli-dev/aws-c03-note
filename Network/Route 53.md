# Amazon Route 53

Route 53 is a highly available, scalable, fully managed, **authoritative** DNS service. Authoritative means you can update the DNS records. Route 53 is also a domain registrar and can check the health of your resources.

## Key Facts

- The only AWS service that provides a 100% availability SLA.
- Named after the traditional DNS port, 53.
- Answers DNS queries with the IP address of your resource; it does not forward application traffic.
