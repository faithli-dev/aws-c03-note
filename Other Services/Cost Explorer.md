# Cost Explorer

Visualise, understand, and manage your AWS costs and usage over time.

## Characteristics

- Create custom reports that analyse cost and usage data.
- Analyse data at a high level (total costs and usage across all accounts) or at monthly, hourly, or resource-level granularity.
- Choose an optimal Savings Plan to lower prices on your bill.
- Forecast usage up to 18 months based on previous usage.

## Views

- Monthly cost by AWS service
- Hourly and resource-level cost
- Savings Plan analysis (an alternative to Reserved Instances)
- Forecast usage
