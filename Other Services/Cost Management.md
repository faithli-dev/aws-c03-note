# AWS Cost Management

## Cost Explorer
- Analyze cost and usage by service, account, Region, tag, resource, and time granularity.
- Create custom reports, forecast future usage, and compare Savings Plans or Reserved Instance options.

## Cost Anomaly Detection
- Uses machine learning to learn historical spending and detect unusual one-time or continuous increases.
- Monitors services, accounts, tags, or cost categories and can notify through SNS with root-cause analysis.

![[SAA-v48-p842-cost-anomaly-detection.png]]

Use tags and Organizations for allocation, Cost Explorer for investigation, Savings Plans or reservations for predictable usage, and anomaly detection to catch unexpected spend.
