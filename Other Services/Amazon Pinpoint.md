# Amazon Pinpoint

A scalable two-way (outbound and inbound) marketing communications service.

## Characteristics

- Supports email, SMS, push, voice, and in-app messaging.
- Segment and personalise messages with the right content for customers.
- Possibility to receive replies.
- Scales to billions of messages per day.

## Use Cases

Run campaigns by sending marketing, bulk, and transactional SMS messages.

## Versus SNS and SES

- With SNS and SES you manage each message's audience, content, and delivery schedule.
- With Pinpoint you create message templates, delivery schedules, highly targeted segments, and full campaigns.
