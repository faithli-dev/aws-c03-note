# AWS Well-Architected Tool

A free tool to review your architectures against the 6 pillars of the Well-Architected Framework and adopt architectural best practices.

## How It Works

1. Select your workload and answer questions.
2. Review your answers against the 6 pillars.
3. Obtain advice: get videos and documentation, generate a report, and see the results in a dashboard.
