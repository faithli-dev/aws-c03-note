# SAA-C03 Exam Review

- Practice scenario questions before the exam and build enough hands-on familiarity to recognize service trade-offs.
- Eliminate answers that violate a requirement, use the wrong service model, or add unnecessary operational complexity.
- Match the answer to the wording: lowest cost, least operational overhead, highest availability, lowest latency, or strongest security each points to a different design.
- Review AWS service FAQs and the Well-Architected and Disaster Recovery whitepapers.
- Know the exam format and current exam guide from the official AWS certification page before booking.

The deck’s strategy is to reason from constraints, choose managed services where appropriate, and verify the smallest architecture that satisfies the scenario.
