# AWS Cloud History

AWS started as Amazon's internal infrastructure and became a public cloud platform. The timeline explains why AWS services are API-first and why infrastructure is treated as a product.

## Timeline

- **2002** – AWS was launched internally at Amazon.
- **2003** – Amazon realised its infrastructure was a core strength and a separate business idea.
- **2004** – Launched publicly with Amazon SQS.
- **2006** – Re-launched publicly with SQS, S3, and EC2.
- **2007** – Launched in Europe.

## Cloud Facts

- AWS revenue was about $90 billion in 2023.
- AWS held roughly 31% of the cloud market in Q1 2024, with Microsoft second at about 25%.
- AWS has been the pioneer and leader of the cloud market for 13 consecutive years.
- More than 1,000,000 active users.
