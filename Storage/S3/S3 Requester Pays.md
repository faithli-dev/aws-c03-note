# S3 Requester Pays

## Default

Bucket owners pay for all Amazon S3 storage and data transfer costs associated with their bucket.

## Requester Pays Buckets

- The requester, instead of the bucket owner, pays the cost of the request and the data download from the bucket.
- Helpful when sharing large datasets with other accounts.
- The requester must be authenticated in AWS; anonymous access is not allowed.
