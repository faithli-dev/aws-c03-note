# Amazon Simple Email Service

Amazon SES is a managed service for sending and receiving email.

- Send transactional or bulk email from applications through the API or SMTP.
- Verify identities and configure domain authentication before sending.
- SES can receive mail, store it in S3, and trigger downstream processing.
- Lambda can call SES from an IAM role, such as sending a welcome email after a DynamoDB Stream event.
